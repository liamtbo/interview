bias: 
    error due to overly simplistic assumptions in the learning algorithm.
variance: 
    error due to the model being too sensitive to small fluctuations in the training data.
low bias, high variance -> overfitting
high bias, low variance -> underfitting

Anomaly detection
    unsupervised/semi-supervised
    Goal: Identify rare, suspicious patterns in unlabeled data (e.g., logs, network traffic).
    Techniques:
        Clustering: K-means 
            (calc dist from each point to it's respective cluster point, points above a threshold distance are outliers)
        Time-series models: predict next value, look at diff of actual vs expected
Threat detection
    supervised learning
    Goal: Classify known threats (e.g., malware, phishing) using labeled data.
    Techniques:
        Binary Classification: Logisitic Regression, Random Forest
